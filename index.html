Services

Three kinds of household magic

Call on one, or invite the whole troupe — most homes start with a single visit and keep us flitting back.

✦

A Spotless House

Sweeping, mopping, dusting, and sanitizing — every surface, seen to.

Tasks that keep the house clean — sweeping, mopping, vacuuming, wiping, dusting; scrubbing and sanitizing the surfaces of the home.

❖

A Kept Home

The everyday upkeep that keeps a home humming along.

Tasks that keep the home running — laundry/linens turnover, dishes, cleaning out and rotating the fridge/pantry, and tidying up high traffic areas; getting the work out of the way so there's time to play!

A Place for Everything and Everything in its Place

If you've lost your home underneath your things, we can help you figure out what you need to keep, what you need to let go, and where to send it. This service usually takes several days to complete, and pricing depends on the density of the space in need. To book this service please select the “other” option and describe your situation.

✿

A Healthy Garden

Mowing, edging, weeding, and pruning to keep things in check.

Tasks that keep your garden under control — mowing, edging, weeding, pruning/shaping, wrangling invasives, and collecting debris for your green bin; so the outside of your home is every bit as welcoming as the inside.
