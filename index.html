Services

Three kinds of household magic

Call on one, or invite the whole troupe — most homes start with a single visit and keep us flitting back.

✦

A Spotless Home

Top-to-bottom cleaning that reaches the corners everyone forgets.

Top-to-bottom cleaning that reaches the corners everyone forgets — kitchens, baths, floors, and all. Book a one-time sparkle or a recurring visit, and come home to a house that practically hums.

❖

Everything in Its Place

Decluttering and organizing systems you can actually keep.

From a single closet to a whole-home reset, we sort, declutter, and conjure up systems you can actually keep. We bring the bins, the labels, and a quiet sense of order that lingers after we go.

A Happy Garden

Mowing, edging, and seasonal tidying for sharp-looking grounds.

Mowing, edging, seasonal tidying, and a little extra fondness for the beds and borders. We keep your grounds looking sharp so the outside of your home is every bit as welcoming as the inside.
